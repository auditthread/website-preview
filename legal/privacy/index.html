Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data AuditThread, Inc. ("AuditThread", "we") collects when you visit auditthread.com, book a demo or get in touch, and what we do with it. Questions go to oleg@auditthread.com.

What we collect

  • When you book a demo: your name, email address and anything you add to the booking. Bookings run through Google Calendar.
  • When you email or meet us: your messages, your contact details and our notes of the conversation.
  • When you visit the site, if you accept analytics cookies: Google Analytics records the pages you view, roughly where you are, your device and browser, and how you found us.

How we use it

We use it to reply to you, run demos, follow up, and see which pages are useful. We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Where UK or EU law applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in running and growing the business, or on steps you ask us to take before a contract. For analytics cookies, we rely on your consent.

Documents you share with us

If you share documents for a demo or pilot, a separate written agreement covers them. Before we process anything, we agree where they're stored and processed, which AI providers see them, who has access and how long we keep them. See Trust and Security.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run AuditThread, such as Google for email, calendar, file storage and analytics. For a pilot, the AI providers named in your agreement. We'll also share data if the law requires it, or with a buyer if AuditThread is ever sold.

Where it's processed

Our providers may process data in the US, the UK, the EU and elsewhere. When data leaves the UK or EU, we rely on their standard contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

Contact and meeting records: while we're in touch, and for up to two years after our last contact. Analytics data: no more than 14 months. You can ask us to delete your data sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll reply within a month. If you're in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

We only use Google Analytics cookies if you click Accept on the cookie banner. They count visits and tell visitors apart. If you click No thanks, we don't load Google Analytics at all.

Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date at the top.
