Trust and Security

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Your SOPs are sensitive. Here's how we look after them.

You choose where documents go

Before a pilot, we agree in writing where your documents are stored and processed, which AI providers see them, who has access and how long we keep them. We can also look at running AuditThread in your own cloud.

AI providers keep no copies

Your documents only go to AI providers that keep no copy, unless you agree to something else in writing. AI providers never train models on your documents.

A person decides every change

AuditThread suggests. Your reviewer accepts, edits or rejects each finding, and every decision records who made it and when.

Nothing is overwritten

Every decision, edit and new version is saved as a new record. The full history of a review is always there to check.

The same model, every time

Each review runs on a fixed, named model version. A new model means a new reviewer version, tested again before anyone uses it.

Your system stays the record

AuditThread doesn't edit your quality system. You get Word files with tracked changes and decide what goes back in. Only named users can see a review.

Data protection

We act as your data processor under a data processing agreement, and give you what you need for your data protection impact assessment.

Questions, or found a security issue? Email oleg@auditthread.com.
